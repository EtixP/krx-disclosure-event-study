# M0.2 — Historically correct transaction-cost model

## Objective

Replace the flat 0.18% sell-tax assumption with an explicit sell-date and
market-aware schedule, without changing event definitions, prices, return
windows, learner features, or the pre-existing +0.30% tradability hurdle.

## State before

`CostModel` exposed a configurable `tx_tax_on_sell=0.0018` and allowed callers
to request a roundtrip cost without a date or market. Every research path then
subtracted the same 0.313% from every event in the 2021–2026 sample. Stored
event-study rows retained t0/t+1/t+2/t+5 prices but not their exact observation
dates, so an exit crossing a January tax boundary could not be priced safely.

The total is equal across KOSPI and KOSDAQ within each encoded period, but its
statutory components differ and are retained separately. The model fails for
markets other than KOSPI/KOSDAQ and for dates outside the law-verified
2021–2026 research interval.

## Design chosen

- `roundtrip_cost` and `net_return` require keyword-only `buy_date`,
  `sell_date`, and `market`.
- Tax selection uses the sell date. Buy date is retained and chronology is
  validated.
- An auditable breakdown separates buy/sell commission, VAT, slippage,
  securities transaction tax, and special rural tax.
- `slippage_bps_per_side` makes the old 5bps assumption unambiguous: 5bps is
  charged on each side, or 10bps roundtrip.
- Commission (0.015% per side), VAT on commission (10%), and slippage remain
  configurable modeling assumptions. They were not tuned in M0.2.
- Costs retain the prior same-notional return-drag approximation. Sell
  notional variation, broker rounding/minimum fees, and measured fills remain
  future execution-model concerns.
- The +0.30% tradability bar is unchanged. It was originally rounded from the
  old 2024-style 0.313% estimate; changing it after seeing corrected results
  would be tuning.

## Execution-date provenance

`MarketDataClient.event_returns` now returns exact t0/t+1/t+2/t+5 observation
dates with the corresponding prices. A strict one-time migration fetched each
stock once and reconstructed the same per-stock observation positions for the
eight primary/legacy event-study files:

- 2,241 stocks fetched;
- 32,450 rows received date fields;
- zero recorded close values were left without their corresponding date;
- existing prices and returns were retained rather than recomputed.

Current research consumers reject missing dates instead of falling back to a
filing-date proxy. The M0.1 pre-revision generator uses an explicitly named
legacy 0.313% constant, so its four historical JSON artifacts still regenerate
byte-for-byte. Its recorded input/source hashes are intentionally historical
and `--check-inputs` now reports M0.2 drift.

## Research consequences

The deterministic comparison is
[`artifacts/m0_2/historical_cost_comparison.json`](../../artifacts/m0_2/historical_cost_comparison.json).

- Category mean net returns decrease by 1.1–1.8 basis points.
- All seven category verdicts remain unchanged.
- Bonus issue moves from 7/10 to 6/10 positive scored windows because a
  near-zero fold crosses zero; its `positive_noisy` verdict is unchanged.
- Buyback realistic mean moves from +0.1572% to +0.1432%; it remains below the
  +0.30% bar.
- Shareholder-change realistic mean moves from −1.0905% to −1.1027%; its
  negative/blacklist conclusion is unchanged and remains provisional pending
  later roadmap methodology corrections.
- Buyback intraday uniform and time-aware levels both fall 1.56bps, while the
  +0.3167% entry-timing delta and fold signs are unchanged.
- The buyback learner is threshold-sensitive: matched-period selection lift
  changes from −1.66bps to +2.23bps and the code-level verdict flips to
  `positive_selective_edge`. This is reported as a fragile secondary result,
  not evidence that the category is now tradable.

No parameter was searched or changed to recover a prior conclusion.

## Handoff invariants

- Never price a research row without exact buy date, exact sell date, and
  KOSPI/KOSDAQ market.
- Preserve KOSPI tax components even when their total equals KOSDAQ.
- Re-verify the open-ended statutory law before extending model support beyond
  2026; do not silently extrapolate.
- Keep the M0.1 pre-revision artifacts historical. Use the M0.2 comparison for
  corrected results.
- Do not reinterpret the independently reproduced learner threshold flip as
  evidence of tradability; it remains small and threshold-sensitive.
