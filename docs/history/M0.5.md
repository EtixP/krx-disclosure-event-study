# M0.5 — Research-inference hardening

## Objective

Add transparent uncertainty and tail diagnostics without turning repeatedly
inspected historical results into confirmatory claims.

## State before M0.5

Headline category results reported event counts, means, descriptive dispersion,
profit factors, and half-year sign breadth. Those summaries treated every row
as a separate observation even though the same issuer can file repeatedly.
The buyback timing discussion had one top-5% diagnostic, but its raw-return
median and trimmed mean were sometimes described next to the abnormal-return
headline without a clear basis label.

The repository also contained many category, horizon, subgroup, learner, and
execution iterations. No untouched holdout remains that could make a current
historical claim confirmatory.

## Chosen inference method

M0.5 uses an issuer-clustered percentile bootstrap:

1. group events by `stock_code`;
2. sample the observed number of issuers with replacement;
3. carry every event from each sampled issuer into the draw;
4. recompute the event-weighted mean;
5. take the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles over 10,000 deterministic draws.

This preserves the existing headline estimand while moving the resampling unit
from event row to issuer. It does not solve every dependence problem: common
event dates, overlapping holding windows, cross-issuer shocks, and the
data-driven choice of hypotheses remain limitations. The intervals are
pointwise descriptive intervals, not familywise confidence statements.

The seven category estimates use realistic T+1-close entry, T+5-close exit,
dated costs, and the fixed KOSPI/KOSDAQ abnormal-return basis. The generator
independently reconstructs each event return and fails unless its count and
mean reconcile with the existing headline analyzer.

## Buyback timing and tails

The current pinned filing-time cohort contains 3,562 events from 937 issuers;
2,625 rows are repeats beyond one row per issuer, and the largest issuer cluster
has 42 events.

| Effect | Mean | Pointwise issuer-clustered 95% CI | Excluding top 5% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| time-aware raw net | +0.458% | [+0.217%, +0.706%] | −0.433% |
| time-aware abnormal net | +0.163% | [−0.059%, +0.395%] | −0.693% |
| paired timing delta, raw | +0.317% | [+0.236%, +0.399%] | −0.031% |
| paired timing delta, abnormal | +0.257% | [+0.184%, +0.331%] | −0.073% |

The paired abnormal delta has a positive pointwise interval, so repeated issuers
alone do not explain the average chronology difference. That does not make it
robust: removing the top 5% of paired deltas flips its sign, the median paired
delta is zero because after-close events use the same entry under both rules,
and the absolute time-aware abnormal-return interval includes zero.

The former `−0.062%` median, `49.4%` win rate, and `−0.433%` top-5%-excluded
mean are raw-return diagnostics. On the abnormal basis those values are
`−0.410%`, `46.1%`, and `−0.693%`. M0.5 records both bases explicitly rather
than using the raw diagnostics to characterize an abnormal headline.

## Hypothesis and data-snooping policy

The report defines three statuses:

- `pre_specified`: hypothesis and rule frozen before evaluation outcomes;
- `exploratory`: formed, selected, or repeatedly examined on the same data;
- `confirmatory`: pre-specified and evaluated once on untouched data.

All current historical category, buyback-timing, shareholder-blacklist, and
subgroup claims are exploratory. There are no current pre-specified or
confirmatory hypotheses. The M0.5 procedure is fixed and reproducible, but that
does not retroactively preregister earlier ideas.

Formal multiple-testing correction is not added to manufacture confirmatory
p-values after the fact. The seven intervals form an explicitly exploratory,
non-adjusted family. A future frozen experiment must define its hypothesis
family and error control before outcomes are observed.

## Validation and handoff invariants

- The cluster bootstrap is seed-stable and invariant when every observation
  within each issuer cluster is duplicated.
- Missing clusters, non-finite outcomes, and fewer than two issuers fail.
- Fixed tail tests report top, bottom, and symmetric exclusions together.
- Every category point estimate reconciles to the current analyzer.
- The report regenerates byte-for-byte and hashes its inputs and sources.
- Verified M0.1–M0.4 artifacts are inputs only as immutable hash invariants.
- Do not describe any current historical subgroup as confirmatory or promote it
  to a strategy from a pointwise interval.

## Independent verification result

Independent verification on 2026-09-04 accepted M0.5 as `VERIFIED`.

- An explicit row-concatenation implementation matched the production
  bootstrap for unequal issuer cluster sizes.
- All seven category returns were independently reconstructed from closes,
  benchmark closes, and a separately encoded dated cost schedule. Alternate
  20,000-draw cluster bootstraps and intercept-only cluster-robust intervals
  preserved every conclusion.
- The buyback timing cohort independently reconciled to 3,562 events and 937
  issuers. A 50,000-draw alternate bootstrap gave approximately
  `[+0.183%, +0.334%]` for the `+0.256729%` paired abnormal delta, consistent
  with the recorded `[+0.184%, +0.331%]` interval.
- Exact sorting reproduced the 178-row top-5% exclusion and its `−0.072804%`
  mean; the absolute abnormal interval still spans zero.
- The artifact regenerated byte-for-byte at `3677a4c2…`, verified upstream
  hashes were unchanged, focused tests passed `5/5`, and all `199` tests passed.
- Documentation accurately treats every current historical claim as
  exploratory. No M1.1 work was introduced.
