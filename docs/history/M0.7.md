# M0.7 — Historical data-readiness audit and ML gate

**Status:** independently verified  
**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Scope:** deterministic audit and decision only

## Objective

M0.6 repaired learner chronology but did not establish that its historical
rows existed in a point-in-time, source-verifiable form. M0.7 determines whether
the current frozen disclosure and daily-price evidence is sufficient to begin
ML development. It does not repair sources, rewrite historical rows, restore or
query the database, train a model, or begin M3.2.

## Evidence boundary

The generator reads exactly eleven hash-pinned local inputs:

- seven category CSVs;
- the benchmark-session CSV and its metadata;
- the immutable buyback filing-time CSV;
- the M0.4 price-adjustment audit, solely for its pinned provider-route and
  adjustment-policy conclusion.

The generator source is also hashed in the artifact. The audit makes no network
or SQLite call. Its reconciliation boundary is the frozen CSV lexemes; it makes
no claim that the upstream DART population or category filters are complete.
Each input byte hash and a permutation-independent category-row lexeme digest
are retained.

The recorded generator command names `.venv/bin/python`, the interpreter that
created the committed bytes, and records that same executable with the runtime
versions. Invoking the recorded command from the repository root reproduces the
artifact byte-for-byte.

features. Event, T0, T+1 and T+5 dates must form an ordered window; T0, T+1 and
T+5 must match the pinned observed benchmark-session horizon; and the T0, T+1
and T+5 stock closes used by M0.6 must be finite and positive. The benchmark horizon is
explicitly described as observed sessions rather than an authoritative exchange
calendar.

## Row copies and overlapping receipts

The 28,979 category-row copies represent 28,737 receipt numbers. At least one
copy is admitted for 28,432 receipts, while every copy is admitted for 28,428.
These populations are separate throughout the artifact.

There are 242 receipts in two categories, yielding 484 retained category
copies. The artifact stores every field lexeme, category, copy hash and
admission result for every one. It selects no canonical copy. Four receipts
have field conflicts and category-dependent admission:

- `20220509900146`: the bonus-issue copy is fetch-failed and missing its window;
  the halt/resumption copy is populated and admitted;
- `20230921900106` and `20230926900688`: bonus-issue copies have T+5 values;
  halt/resumption copies do not;
- `20250124900682`: the rights-offering copy has a T+5 close; the
  convertible-bond copy does not.

No pinned evidence establishes which divergent copy is canonical, so
`cross_category_copy_conflict` remains an independent gate defect.

## Evidence pillars

The frozen category files contain retrospective corporation, stock-code and
market fields. They do not supply an effective-dated security mapping with
instrument type, listing/delisting intervals, code support and event-date
trading eligibility. Seven row copies use six-character alphanumeric stock
codes, further demonstrating that prospective digit-only schemas cannot stand
in for historical identity evidence.

The files contain T0, T+1, T+2 and T+5 closes, but not raw OHLCV response bytes,
response hashes, capture timestamps, request boundaries, volume or adjustment
vintages. The pinned M0.4 conclusion identifies the route as Naver Finance via
pykrx with `adjusted=True`; that retrospective conclusion does not create a
versioned raw-bar source.

The buyback filing-time input supplies exact times for 3,604 of 4,846 rows: 52
before 09:00, 1,703 from 09:00 through 15:29, 11 at exactly 15:30, and 1,838
after 15:30. The other 1,242 remain unavailable. A filing at or after 15:30
cannot justify the same-day close as an executable entry.

The pinned audited inputs do not supply exact original document-version bytes,
document hashes, relationship captures or contemporaneous observation times.
Those fields are reported as `not_supplied_by_pinned_inputs`; the audit does not
inspect the current database to infer them.

## Gate decision

The artifact returns `NO_GO` with the fixed ordered blocker codes:

1. `missing_point_in_time_security_identity`;
2. `missing_point_in_time_disclosure_evidence`;
3. `missing_versioned_daily_label_source`;
4. `cross_category_copy_conflict`.

`GO` requires every admitted category-row copy to satisfy all three evidence
pillars and requires no unresolved copy conflict. Historical intraday evidence
is reported separately as
`historical_intraday_unavailable_prospective_only`. It is informational and is
not treated as a substitute for the daily-label pillar.

This result does not end the ML branch. The next bounded milestone should test
source feasibility for effective-dated security identity and a versioned raw
daily outcome source for buybacks. Transformer or document-derived features
should wait for exact document-version bytes, lineage and decision-time
availability. Model training should wait until the resulting rows pass the
gate.
